# Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard – Excel

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify business trends, evaluate sales performance, understand discount impact, and generate actionable business recommendations.

An interactive Sales Performance Dashboard was developed using Excel PivotTables, PivotCharts, formulas, and data analysis techniques to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Evaluate Online vs. Retail sales performance.
- Identify top-selling and lowest-revenue products.
- Analyse sales and profitability across product categories.
- Understand the relationship between discounts, sales, and profit.
- Identify business problems and provide practical recommendations.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Excel formulas
- Data analysis and visualization
- Conditional formatting
- Data filtering and sorting

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains 24 sales transactions covering January to March 2026.

## Excel Workbook Structure

The workbook contains two worksheets:

### 1. Dashboard

The dashboard presents sales analysis through PivotTables and charts.

**Analysis included:**

- Sales by Region
- Online vs. Retail Sales
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Discount Category
- Top-Selling Products by Units Sold
- Top Products by Net Sales
- Lowest-Revenue Products
- Units Sold by Discount Category
- Net Sales by Discount Category
- Profit by Discount Category

It also includes business insights and practical business recommendations based on the analysis.

### 2. Sales Data

Contains the original sales transactions and calculated fields, including:

- Gross Sales
- Discount Amount
- Total Cost
- Net Sales
- Profit or Loss

## Key Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important business observations:

1. **Discount Impact:** Higher discount categories are associated with negative profitability in the dataset.
2. **Regional Performance:** The South region recorded the lowest net sales.
3. **Product Performance:** Storage Box recorded the highest unit sales, while some products recorded relatively low sales quantities.
4. **Category Performance:** Fashion generated the highest net sales, while Electronics generated the lowest.
5. **Sales Channels:** Online and Retail channels contributed almost equally to net sales.
6. **Profitability:** Some products generated revenue but recorded negative profits, highlighting the importance of monitoring product-level profitability.

## Practical Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

- Control excessive discounts to reduce losses.
- Use moderate discounts strategically to maintain profitability.
- Run targeted marketing campaigns for low-selling products.
- Review pricing and supplier costs for loss-making products.
- Improve marketing and product availability in the South region.
- Maintain adequate inventory for high-demand products.
- Improve Electronics category performance.
- Monitor the profitability of Online and Retail sales channels.

## Project Limitations

- The analysis is based on 24 transactions covering approximately three months.
- The findings represent patterns in the available dataset and may not represent long-term business performance.
- The relationship between discounts and profitability does not independently establish causation.
- More historical data would help validate the findings and improve business decisions.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

By analysing sales trends, regional performance, product demand, discount strategies, and profitability, businesses can identify improvement opportunities and make more informed decisions.
